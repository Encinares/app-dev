# My Favorite Series: #Alive

**Genre:** Thriller, Horror, Drama  
**Country:** South Korea  
**Directed by:** Cho Il-hyung  
**Starring:** Yoo Ah-in, Park Shin-hye  

---

# Story Summary
#Alive is a Korean survival thriller film where a deadly virus outbreak spreads rapidly in Seoul, turning people into violent zombies. The story follows **Oh Joon-woo**, a gamer trapped inside his apartment, struggling to survive as communication and electricity are cut off. 

Despite isolation and fear, Joon-woo later discovers another survivor, **Kim Yoo-bin**, living in the building opposite his. Together, they find hope and courage to keep fighting for survival.

---

# Why I Like It
I really like *#Alive* because it shows how people stay strong even when they’re alone and scared. The story combines fear, hope, and teamwork — reminding us to never give up even in the hardest times.

---

# Favorite Moments
- When Joon-woo finds out he’s not alone anymore  
- The teamwork between Joon-woo and Yoo-bin  
- The ending escape scene filled with tension and hope
